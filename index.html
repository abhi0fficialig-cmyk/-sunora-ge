What is PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana?

Launched by the Government of India, this scheme helps households install rooftop solar and enjoy up to 300 units of free electricity every month, with a central subsidy credited directly to your bank account. The goal: clean solar power for one crore homes across India.

How much subsidy can you get?

Eligible households can receive up to ₹78,000 in central subsidy: around ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the 3rd kW. Our experts help you size your system right and claim the maximum subsidy you qualify for.

know before you buy

Solar systems explained.

On-grid, off-grid or hybrid? Here is a simple guide to how each system works, what it costs and who it is best for, so you can pick the one that fits your home.

On-Grid Solar System

Panels power your home by day and send the extra to the grid. The net-meter credits every unit you export, so your bill drops to near zero.

Best for Homes, shops and factories with a reliable grid

Off-Grid Solar System

A fully independent plant: panels charge a battery bank that runs your loads day and night, with no grid connection needed.

Best for Farms, remote sites and areas with long power cuts

Hybrid Solar System

Grid savings plus battery backup in one system. Use solar first, store the extra and keep the lights on during power cuts.

Best for Homes and offices that face regular outages
